# Third-party assets

## Kenney Blaster Kit 2.1

The 3D blasters, washer and grenade in `assets/3d/tools/` come from
[Kenney's Blaster Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit).

Kenney released these files under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). The
original license notice is preserved at
`assets/3d/KENNEY-BLASTER-KIT-LICENSE.txt`.

The models have been renamed and are rendered with custom positioning,
lighting, perspective and animation for Unwind.

## OpenGameArt CC0 models

- `chainsaw.gltf` comes from [Chainsaw by loafbrr_1](https://opengameart.org/content/chainsaw-1), released under CC0. The supplied license notice is preserved at `assets/3d/CHAINSAW-CC0-LICENSE.txt`.
- `bat.glb` comes from [Old Wooden Baseball Bat by LonesomeDucky](https://opengameart.org/content/old-wooden-baseball-bat), released under CC0.

Both models are rendered with Unwind's own scale, lighting, inertia, perspective and impact animation.

## Three.js

The 3D renderer uses [Three.js](https://threejs.org/), distributed under the
MIT License. Runtime modules are bundled locally so the game works offline.
