ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Made to help you leave a little lighter.

Unwind is a free, offline-friendly desktop playground created and directed by Vivek. It is intended as a playful moment of release, with no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or paid features.

Inspiration

Unwind was inspired by the spirit of classic desktop-destruction games, including the old Desktop Destroyer style of play. It is an independent project with its own interface, code, artwork, tools, effects, and sound design. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or presented as an official continuation of any earlier game.

Safety and privacy

Unwind only changes its own simulated canvas. It does not damage files, control other applications, or upload screenshots. Screen capture creates a temporary still replica in memory. Hide private information before capturing a screen.
